Bibliotecas de audio BASS incluidas en zRadio BASS
==================================================

Esta carpeta contiene las versiones de 64 bits (x64) para Windows de:

  bass.dll      BASS 2.4.16.7       (biblioteca principal)
  bassenc.dll   BASSenc 2.4.16.1    (grabación a WAV)
  bass_fx.dll   BASS_FX 2.4.12.6    (ecualizador)
  bass_aac.dll  BASS_AAC 2.4.3.0    (emisoras AAC / AAC+)
  bassflac.dll  BASSFLAC 2.4.4.5    (emisoras FLAC)
  bassopus.dll  BASSOPUS 2.4.2.1    (emisoras Opus)
  basshls.dll   BASSHLS 2.4.5.0     (emisoras HLS, .m3u8)
  basswma.dll   BASSWMA 2.4.4.0     (emisoras WMA)
